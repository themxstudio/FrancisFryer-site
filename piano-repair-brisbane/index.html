Repair service

Careful fault-finding and practical piano repair work

Each repair visit starts with a clear look at the instrument so the work matches the real issue. That keeps repair decisions sensible, avoids guesswork, and helps you understand the condition of the piano before bigger work is recommended.

Keys, pedals, and action problems

Sticky keys, uneven touch, noisy action parts, worn felt, and pedal problems can all be inspected and addressed with careful mechanical repair work.

Strings, rattles, and internal faults

Broken strings, buzzing notes, loose fittings, and other internal issues can often be repaired or stabilised without unnecessary replacement work.

Clear advice before larger repairs

If the piano needs more extensive work, you get straightforward advice on condition, likely cost, and whether the repair is worth doing.
