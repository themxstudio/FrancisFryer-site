Tuning service

Tuning that improves stability, balance, and playing feel

Whether the piano needs routine maintenance or has been left too long between visits, the goal is the same: bring the instrument back to a reliable, musical pitch without rushing the process.

Routine maintenance tuning

Standard tuning appointments are ideal for pianos that have been maintained within the usual 6–12 month window and need a careful reset of pitch and balance.
